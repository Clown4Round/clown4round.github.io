# Privacy Policy for UnderSpeak

**Last Updated:** October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UnderSpeak ("the bot") handles, processes, and protects your information. By adding UnderSpeak to your server or interacting with its features, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Process (Transient Data)
UnderSpeak functions as a Text-to-Speech (TTS) entertainment style bot. To perform its core features, it dynamically processes data in real-time:
* **Message Content & Length:** The bot transiently reads alphanumeric text characters sent in designated text channels. It utilizes the length and formatting of these characters purely to calculate and synchronize corresponding Undertale character sound blips in your voice channel. 
* **Data Deletion:** This message content is processed in volatile memory (RAM) and is completely discarded immediately after the audio blips are queued or played. We never store, log, or record the contents of your text messages or voice chats on any disk or database.

## 2. Information We Store (Persistent Data)
To maintain user customization and server layouts, the bot saves minimal configuration settings to a secure database. This data includes:
* **User IDs:** Stored only if a user interacts with personalized configurations (such as setting up a custom character voice profile via slash commands).
* **Guild (Server) and Channel IDs:** Stored to remember bot settings, designated text-to-speech tracking channels, and configuration setups for individual servers.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
The minimal data collected is used strictly to:
* Provide the core functionality of the bot (playing audio blips in voice channels).
* Remember and apply your personalized character voice profiles across servers.
* Maintain server-specific administrative configurations.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user or server data with third parties.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
* Configuration data (user profiles and server settings) is retained for as long as the bot is active on your server or until your profile is updated.
* If you wish to have your stored configuration data manually deleted from our database, please contact us through our official channels.

## 5. Third-Party Services
UnderSpeak is built using the Discord API. Your data usage is also subject to the [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com) and [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com).

## 6. Contact & Support
If you have any questions or data removal requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our support server or open an issue on our official project repository.
